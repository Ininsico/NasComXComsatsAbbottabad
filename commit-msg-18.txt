Link the CRM workspace into the app

The engine from the previous commit was only reachable over HTTP. This wires it
into the signed-in app as four real pages.

- /crm: pipeline board, one column per stage, with a live per-lead
  acknowledgement countdown, escalation badges, KPI tiles and an inline
  create-lead form. Polls every 5s so an escalation appears without a reload.
- /crm/:id: lead detail — acknowledge, move stage, reassign, record activity,
  and book a Google Calendar appointment with conflict checking.
- /integrations: Google Calendar connect/disconnect plus the real redirect URI to
  register, alert channel configuration (webhook/Slack/Telegram), and the alert
  attempt history including "skipped" runs.
- /settings: acknowledgement window, per-member open-lead limits, the escalation
  ladder as absolute offsets from the deadline, and member availability.

Signing in now lands on the pipeline instead of the old dashboard, and the
sidebar carries Pipeline, Integrations and Workspace for every role.

Also fixes the demo seeder: it re-applied its default settings on every boot,
which would silently undo anything configured on the settings page. It now only
seeds when no settings row exists.

checks against the live API for every endpoint these pages call.
