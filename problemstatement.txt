Build a multi-tenant SaaS CRM for 5–30-employee service businesses.

Each company is a fully isolated workspace with three base levels:

- **Owner** — invites users, assigns levels, grants/removes configurable permissions for
  view/create/edit/delete/export on CRM records, manage assignment rules, manage
  integrations, view the audit log. Can never be modified by an Admin.
- **Admin** — delegated configurer who reshapes pipelines, custom fields, assignment rules
  and integrations without code.
- **User** — handles only assigned leads/conversations within their permissions.

Plus a configurable "Manager" role built from permission bundles rather than a hard-coded
fourth level.

Field-level masking of opportunity value in lists, detail pages and exports. Server-side
enforcement on every endpoint (hiding a button is never sufficient). Workspace isolation
that survives guessed identifiers.

The CRM stores Contacts (belonging to a Company, with many Opportunities), Companies and
Opportunities, on a customizable pipeline whose stages an Admin can add/rename/reorder/
delete (safely, even with opportunities inside) and between which opportunities move on a
board or list, blocked with a clear message when stage-specific required fields are
missing. Admin-created custom fields (text/number/date/dropdown/checkbox) appear
immediately on forms and detail pages, differing per pipeline while sharing the same
contact data. Saved lists combining at least three criteria (field, tag, stage, owner,
date) that auto-update as records start or stop matching. A time-ordered per-record
activity history of notes, stage changes, assignments, field edits, messages, payments and
appointments, with actor and timestamp. One complete contact page showing conversations,
opportunities, payments, appointments, the assigned employee, and the activity history.

Lead protection
---------------

Leads are captured from a public web form, a Twilio WhatsApp/SMS sandbox inbound message,
and manual entry, each carrying a source label. They are assigned by Admin-configured
round-robin only to employees whose status is Available and whose open-lead count is under
the configured cap (Busy/On leave/Offline never receive leads; setting On leave/Offline
immediately reassigns unacknowledged leads per a visible stated policy for
already-acknowledged ones), with a visible acknowledgement countdown (acknowledged =
clicked Acknowledge OR any recorded action: note, message, call log, stage change,
appointment) that, when it expires, escalates through a configurable chain (next eligible
rep → designated backup → manager, number of levels and per-level time configurable in
seconds for demo), fires a real external alert via a chosen provider (Slack/Telegram/
transactional email) to the new owner's per-user destination within the same minute,
records failed alerts with limited retries visible to Admins, and guarantees a lead is
never owned by two people at once and never double-escalated when two timers expire close
together.

A recovery queue page lists every lead unacknowledged beyond its window, escalated two or
more times, or untouched for the configured idle period, with the reason shown per item.

Integrations (all sandbox/test mode)
------------------------------------

**Twilio WhatsApp/SMS**
- Signature-verified webhooks (forged requests rejected and recorded).
- Inbound messages matching known numbers to existing contacts, or creating contact + lead
  from unknown numbers.
- Outbound sends with sent/delivered/failed statuses stored and shown.
- Retry on failed sends, with nothing silently dropped.
- Webhook duplicate deliveries creating neither duplicate messages nor duplicate leads.
- Opted-out contacts blocked from messaging, with the reason explained.

**Stripe test mode**
- Payment links/checkout sessions from an opportunity for a stated amount and currency.
- Payments confirmed via signature-verified idempotent webhooks, with no manual refresh.
- Expired/abandoned/failed/refunded statuses shown correctly, including refunds made in the
  Stripe dashboard.
- Collected revenue (webhook-confirmed minus confirmed refunds, used only for "payments
  received" on the dashboard) distinguished from projected revenue (open opportunities plus
  unpaid payment requests).
- Each payment traced to its opportunity, contact, requesting employee and provider
  reference.
- Card numbers never stored or logged.

**Google Calendar via OAuth 2.0**
- Per-employee consent to connect/disconnect.
- Automatic access-token refresh; server-only token storage.
- Detection of revoked/expired connections, shown as "needs reconnection".
- Appointment booking from an opportunity or contact that appears in the employee's real
  Google Calendar and on the customer record.
- Busy-time conflict prevention or warning.
- Unambiguous timezone-safe storage.
- Reschedule/cancel syncing the Google event and recording it in the activity history.
- An employee without a connected calendar cannot be booked, and is shown why.

**Governance**
- A central integrations page listing each integration with status (not connected /
  connected / error), last-worked time, and recent errors.
- Per-workspace credentials, so one workspace's connection is never used by another.
- Secrets never sent to the browser, committed to the repo, or shown in full in the UI.
- A synchronization history recording every inbound event and outbound call with
  time/result/reference, reviewable by authorized users.
- Retry-on-temporary versus plain-explanation-on-permanent error handling.
- Clearly stated per-integration limits (e.g. a WhatsApp sandbox only reaches joined
  phones).
- Permission-gated and audited connection changes.
- Failure isolation so one provider being down leaves the rest of the platform working.

Audit log
---------

A tamper-resistant log recording assignment, acknowledgement, escalation, reassignment,
role/permission changes, field/pipeline configuration changes, integration connection
changes, and deletions, with actor-or-system, time, target, and before/after values where
relevant. Visible only to users with the audit permission. Filterable by event type, user
and date. It cannot be edited or deleted by any screen or endpoint.

Operational dashboard
---------------------

Shows new leads today, open pipeline value by stage, average time to first response (first
assignment to first acknowledgement), recovery-queue count, escalations in the last 24
hours, employee availability, upcoming appointments, and payments received this month
(collected revenue only), where every figure follows the Section 7 glossary definitions and
matches the underlying records when opened. At least three figures drill down to filtered
record lists the user is allowed to see. Users see personal figures while Owner/Admin see
company-wide, and show/hide choices persist.

Interlinking
------------

The whole thing is one navigable product:

- Login lands on the dashboard.
- Dashboard drill-downs reach Leads, Opportunities, Recovery Queue, Appointments, Payments,
  Contact detail, Opportunity detail and Lead detail.
- Contacts list → Contact detail → Company detail, Opportunity detail, Conversation view,
  Payment detail, Appointment, and Activity history.
- Opportunities board → Opportunity detail → Contact, Payment request, Booking, Activity
  history.
- Leads list → Lead detail → Contact, Conversation, Reassign, Acknowledge.
- Recovery queue → Lead detail.
- Conversations inbox → Conversation view → Contact, Lead (when new), Opportunity.
- Payments list → Payment detail → Opportunity, Contact.
- Booking screen → Opportunity, Contact, Google Calendar status.
- Escalation alerts deep-link to Lead detail.
- Per-user notification routing lives in settings.
- The audit log is written by every important action.
- Settings hosts Members, Roles/Permissions, Pipeline config, Custom fields, Assignment
  rules, Notification routing and Integrations.

Excluded
--------

The public marketing site, platform subscription/billing, full accounting (ledgers,
reconciliation, financial statements), payroll, shift scheduling, legal leave/break rules,
ad-platform analytics/campaign attribution, a full report builder/BI, and training/
onboarding modules.

Delivery
--------

Ships with written startup instructions, an env template, and seed/sample data so a new
person can run the app from scratch.
